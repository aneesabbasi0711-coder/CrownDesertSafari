Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Who we are

Crown Desert Safari operates desert safari and city tour experiences in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and Sharjah, United Arab Emirates. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website or book a tour with us, how we use it, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, contact us on WhatsApp or by phone and we will explain it in plain terms.

2. Information we collect

When you use our booking form or contact us directly, we may collect:

  • Your full name and contact number
  • Your email address, if you provide one
  • Your pickup address or hotel name
  • Your preferred tour date, package selection, and number of travelers
  • Any message or special request you add to the booking form

We do not collect payment card details through our website. All bookings are confirmed through WhatsApp or the booking form, and payment is arranged directly and does not pass through our site.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Confirm your booking and arrange hotel pickup and drop-off
  • Contact you on WhatsApp, phone, or email about your tour
  • Respond to questions or special requests
  • Improve our packages and customer service

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Sharing your information

We share limited booking details, such as your name, contact number, and pickup location, with the drivers and desert camp staff who deliver your tour, so they can meet you at the correct time and place. We do not share your information with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

5. WhatsApp communication

Our primary booking channel is WhatsApp. When you message us or submit the booking form, a WhatsApp conversation is created between you and our team. Your messages are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy in addition to this one.

6. Cookies and website analytics

Our website does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking scripts. If we introduce analytics tools in the future to understand how visitors use our site, we will update this policy to describe exactly what is collected and give you the option to opt out where required by law.

7. Photos and videos taken during tours

Photos or videos taken by our team during a tour may occasionally be used for marketing on our website or social media. If you do not want to appear in marketing material, tell your guide on the day or message us on WhatsApp and we will exclude your images.

8. Data retention

We keep booking information for as long as needed to deliver your tour and to meet our own record-keeping and legal obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

9. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Contact us using the details below and we will respond promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.

11. Contact us

For any privacy question, reach us on WhatsApp or phone at +971 54 150 8706 or by email at info.crowndesertsafari@gmail.com.
